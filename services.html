How we scope an engagement

Most brands come to us for one thing and stay for the reporting.

There is no fixed package. After the account review we propose a scope that matches where your account actually loses money — for some brands that is advertising, for others it is catalogue hygiene and fee errors that nobody has looked at in two years.

What does not change: we work inside your own Seller Central and Amazon Ads accounts, you keep ownership of everything, and the monthly contribution statement is included whatever else we are doing.

  • Retainer based on catalogue size, marketplaces and scope — never a percentage of ad spend
  • Rolling contract after the first three months
  • Named lead who runs the account, not a rotating junior
  • Daily ASIN monitoring through Seller Control Hub on every managed listing
