How an engagement starts

No long tie-in before anyone has looked at your data. The first fortnight is diagnosis.

  1. Account review

    You grant read access to Seller Central and Amazon Ads. We go through the catalogue, advertising structure, indexing, fee and reimbursement position, and build the current contribution picture by SKU.

  2. Findings and plan

    A written review of what we found, what it is costing, and what we would do in the first 90 days — in priority order, with the expected effect on margin. Yours to keep whether or not you hire us.

  3. Fix the foundations

    Before scaling spend: indexing, variation families, suppressed and stranded stock, broken content, fee and FNSKU errors, reimbursement claims. This is usually where the first month's gain comes from.

  4. Run and report

    Daily ASIN monitoring, weekly advertising work against target margin, monthly contribution statement and a call on what it tells us. Quarterly: range, pricing and market decisions.

Questions brands ask before they switch agency

What does a full-service Amazon agency actually manage?

Everything that moves the numbers inside Seller Central: advertising, listing content and keyword indexing, catalogue and variation structure, pricing and Buy Box, stock cover and replenishment, cases and account health, and the reporting that ties it together. We work inside your own Seller Central and Amazon Ads accounts, so you keep ownership of the account and the data.

What size of brand do you work with?

Established brands, typically turning over £1m or more a year on Amazon, or approaching it. We keep the client list deliberately short so the people who audit your account are the people who run it.

Do you report on profit or just on sales and ACoS?

Both. Alongside the usual advertising metrics you get a monthly contribution statement per SKU and per marketplace — revenue, Amazon fees, advertising, cost of goods, returns and reimbursements, down to contribution profit and margin. It is the reason most clients come to us.

Can you manage Amazon US and EU as well as Amazon UK?

Yes. We manage UK, US and European marketplaces, including the listing, compliance, tax-registration sequencing and advertising differences between them. See international expansion.

How are you different from a PPC-only agency?

A PPC agency can only change bids. Most wasted spend on Amazon comes from problems upstream of the ad account — a listing that is not indexed for its best keyword, a broken variation family, a stock-out that resets rank. We fix those as part of the same engagement.

How do you charge?

A monthly retainer based on catalogue size, number of marketplaces and how much of the account we manage. No percentage of ad spend, which would pay us to spend more of your money. Fees are quoted after the account review, in writing.
