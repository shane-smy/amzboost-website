A small Amazon agency, run by people who have sold on it

AMZ Boost is based in Ipswich, Suffolk, and works with a deliberately short list of established brands on Amazon UK, US and Europe. Fifteen years of selling on Amazon, more than $100m in sales across the brands we have managed, and a habit of doing the monthly accounts as well as the marketing — which is why the reporting looks the way it does.

Why we work this way

Most Amazon agencies are built to scale: a sales team at the front, a growing client list, and account managers handling as many brands each as the model requires. It works, and it is why so many brands have been through three agencies in five years.

We went the other way. A short client list, senior people on every account, and growth that comes from the accounts getting better rather than from adding more of them. The person who audits your account is the person who runs it, and you have their number.

The other difference is background. We did not arrive at Amazon from advertising. We arrived from selling, and from the monthly job of working out what the business actually made after Amazon had taken its share. That habit turned into the contribution reporting every client now gets, and it is the thing brands mention most when they move to us.

What we bring to an account

Seller Control Hub

Our own monitoring software, built because the alternatives watched the wrong things. It checks every ASIN we manage daily for Buy Box loss, suppressions, content overwrites, price moves and stock risk, so problems surface the morning they happen.

Amazon Ads Verified Partner

AMZ Boost is a verified partner in Amazon's advertising partner programme, which means Amazon has checked our credentials against our managed accounts rather than taking our word for it.

An in-house PPC specialist

Advertising is run by a full-time specialist on our team, not outsourced and not handled by whoever has capacity. Bids and listings are managed by people who speak to each other daily.

Financial reporting as standard

Monthly contribution statements by SKU and marketplace, reconciled to settlement reports. Included in every engagement, and available on its own.

In the room where it changes

We attend Amazon Accelerate and sit in mastermind groups with large brands, which is how we see policy shifts, fee changes and new advertising formats before they reach the general seller population.
